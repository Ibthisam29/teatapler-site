Membership · Essentials

How does the subscription work?

Each month, your tier's selection ships automatically. You'll be charged on the same date each billing cycle.

Can I pause or cancel?

Yes — pause for up to 3 months or cancel anytime from your account. No commitment required.

When will I be charged?

Your first charge happens at signup. Monthly members are charged on the same day each month; yearly members once annually with 10% savings.

Where do you ship?

Classic ships domestically free. Reserve and Atelier include complimentary global shipping.
